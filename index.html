OBJECT FILE 001 · THE MOON

The Part of Apollo That Stayed Behind

The Apollo Lunar Module had two stages. The descent stage helped the astronauts land on the Moon and served as their launch platform for the journey back to lunar orbit.

When the ascent stage lifted off, the descent stage remained on the lunar surface. It was not simply forgotten: leaving it behind was part of the mission design.

OBJECT FILE 002 · MARS

Sojourner: A Small Rover, a Giant Step

NASA's Sojourner rover explored the Martian surface as part of the Pathfinder mission in 1997. It tested how a small mobile robot could examine rocks and soil on another planet.

Sojourner completed its mission on Mars. The rover and Pathfinder lander remained there, leaving behind a lasting record of an important step in robotic exploration.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Abandoned,
but Not Forgotten.

Space exploration is often remembered through launches, first landings and extraordinary discoveries. But there is another story: the machines and instruments that remain after their missions end.

This project explores their purpose, achievements and legacy through historical mission information and official space-agency sources.

The mission may end. Its story does not.
