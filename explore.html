THE LEGACY OF SPACE EXPLORATION

A DIFFERENT SIDE OF EXPLORATION

The Mission Ends. The Evidence Remains.

Space missions are remembered for their launches, discoveries and historic moments. But every mission also leaves behind a physical record of the journey.

Some equipment is left by design, such as the lower stages of lunar landers. Other machines remain where their missions ended, including robotic landers and rovers on Mars.

These objects are not just forgotten machines. They are part of the history of how we explored other worlds.

OBJECT FILE 001 · THE MOON

A Landing Place That Became History.

The Moon holds the remains of several crewed and robotic missions. Some objects were left there to make the journey home possible; others were scientific equipment.

APOLLO LUNAR MODULE

The Part of Apollo That Stayed Behind

The Lunar Module had two parts. Its descent stage carried astronauts down to the lunar surface and provided the platform from which the ascent stage could lift off again.

When astronauts departed the Moon, the descent stage remained at the landing site. Leaving it behind was part of the mission design, not an accident.

A RECORD THROUGH TIME

Milestones That Left a Mark.

A short timeline of missions whose equipment became part of the story of lunar and Martian exploration.

1969

Apollo 11

The first crewed Moon landing. The Lunar Module's descent stage remained at the Sea of Tranquility.

1971

Apollo 15

The mission used the Lunar Roving Vehicle, which was left on the Moon when the astronauts returned.

1976

Viking 1 and Viking 2

NASA's Viking landers reached Mars and carried out experiments on the planet's surface.

1997

Mars Pathfinder

Pathfinder delivered Sojourner, the first rover to operate on the surface of Mars.

2004

Spirit and Opportunity

The twin Mars rovers explored the planet for years, far longer than their original planned missions.

A LEGACY BEYOND THE MISSION

What We Left Behind

Every space mission leaves a mark. From abandoned equipment on the Moon to robotic explorers on Mars, these objects preserve the history of our journey beyond Earth.

01 / THE MOON

Footprints That Remain

The Apollo missions left behind lunar module descent stages, scientific instruments and other equipment. These objects remain at the historic landing sites, preserving evidence of humanity's first journeys to the Moon.

02 / MARS

Machines on the Red Planet

Mars has its own collection of mission remains. Landers and rovers such as Viking, Pathfinder, Spirit and Opportunity explored the planet and left hardware behind after their missions.
