ABOUT THE PROJECT

Some of the most important explorers are the ones we left behind.

Abandoned but Not Forgotten is an educational project dedicated to the spacecraft, rovers, landers, and scientific equipment that remain on other worlds after their missions.

These machines travelled millions of miles, explored unfamiliar landscapes, collected valuable information, and helped expand our understanding of the universe. Their missions may have ended, but their stories have not.

THE REMAINS OF EXPLORATION
THE STORY BEHIND IT

Why Do We Remember Abandoned Equipment?

Space missions are designed to explore places that humans cannot easily reach. Many spacecraft and instruments are not designed to return to Earth.

When their work is complete, they can remain on the surfaces they helped us understand. They become physical records of scientific ambition, engineering, and discovery.

This project brings attention to these forgotten objects and the important roles they played.

OUR PURPOSE

Making Forgotten History Visible Again.

Our goal is to make the history of abandoned space equipment easier to discover and understand. By bringing mission stories, historical information, and visual references together, we hope to show that space exploration is about more than just the moments of launch and landing.

It is also about the discoveries, challenges, technology, and objects that remain long after a mission has finished.

04 / A LASTING LEGACY

The Mission May End. The Discovery Lives On.

Every rover, lander, and instrument has contributed something to our understanding of space. Even when equipment stops operating, the knowledge gained through its mission remains valuable.

These objects remind us how far curiosity and human imagination can take us.
